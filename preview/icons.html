Your art, extracted

Pulled off the contact sheet at its native resolution: the art is drawn on a 6×6 pixel grid with its origin at (1,2), so each authored pixel was sampled once rather than averaged. Native icons come out at most 40×40. Our own two sets are dropped.

Round-trip check: scaling the extraction back up by 6 and comparing against the source gives a mean channel error of 6.3/255. Not zero, because the sheet is a soft-edged render rather than a clean integer upscale - but low enough that the grid is right. A wrong block size lands several times higher.

The wither is wider than it is tall

Its three skulls are 39-40 native pixels across but only 20-24 tall, so it never fits a square slot the way the others do. Two ways out, both above: full keeps all three skulls and letterboxes them with transparent padding top and bottom - which needs the 48px art size, since at 32px the outer skulls get cut. Centre skull crops to the middle head and fits any size, at the cost of the wither's most recognisable feature. At 32px art the full version is unusable - the outer skulls get sliced - so 32px ships the centre skull and 48px ships all three.
