Boss checklist, built from the game's own art

Every icon is the classic inventory slot (gui/sprites/container/slot.png, exactly 18×18: a 16×16 interior inside a 1px bevel) with a real vanilla item sprite centred in it. Locked is that sprite as a flat #3b3b3b silhouette, the recipe-book treatment for something you have not unlocked.

Three decisions on this page, independent of each other: how big, what shape, and which item per boss.

Size and shape

Usable width per side is 115px (+125 to +240). Each option below is shown at actual size against a dark scene and against daylight grass, with the real hotbar next to it at true scale - that adjacency is the whole test. Every mock is the same mixed state: dragon and wither unlocked, warden and elder still locked.

Four across at 32px art would be 144px wide and does not fit the 115px budget, so it is not offered. 1.5x cannot map pixels evenly - some source pixels become 2 wide and some stay 1, so the art comes out visibly lumpy. 1x and 2x are clean.

Native higher-resolution art - genuinely more detail at 32px rather than scaled-up 16px - is not here on purpose. Vanilla has no source above 16×16, so it would mean hand-drawing the icons again, which is exactly what got rejected. Everything below is real vanilla art, scaled.
